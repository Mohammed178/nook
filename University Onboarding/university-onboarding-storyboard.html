Nook · Product Storyboard

Universities list their own housing

An onboarding journey that brings university housing offices onto Nook as first-class listers — verified not by paperwork, but by a Nook admin reaching the university through its own official channels.

The payoff is a trust signal students can't get anywhere else: Listed by the university — the room is offered by the campus itself, not a middleman.

Why the flow is shaped this way

Verify the institution, not the paperwork

Agents prove themselves with a BOVAEP licence. A university's proof is that it answers its own switchboard. One outbound call to a publicly published number beats any uploaded document — documents can be forged; the campus phone line can't.

One account per university

The account is the university's public identity on Nook. A single verified account means students never have to wonder which "UM" is the real one — and one switchboard call verifies it.

Call the published number, never the applicant's

The number in the application could belong to anyone. The number on the university's own website belongs to the university. Outreach always starts from the official source.

Approval leaves a receipt

Every university approval records who was reached, on which number, on what date. Trust that can be audited is trust that keeps.

Reuse the rails

Universities ride the same dashboard, listing tools, and approval queue agents already use. New for them; battle-tested for us. The onboarding is new — almost nothing else is.

The badge is the product

Everything upstream exists so a student can see one pill and relax. "Listed by the university" only means something because steps 4–6 actually happened.
